Bekai Vision

Computer vision & robotics engineering — from research prototype to production deployment.

Bekai Vision is an independent computer vision and robotics engineering studio founded by Alibek Erkabayev (GitHub · personal site). We help teams turn visual data into working systems — from the first proof-of-concept to optimized models running on cloud and edge hardware.

Services

What we're building

in development

AnchorAR

AR tracking library, planar targets first. Written in C++20 with a WebAssembly build. Early stage.

in development

RescueSwarm

Research project: a simulated mixed swarm of drones and ground robots that builds a shared map of an unknown disaster zone and marks where people may be found. Built on ROS 2 Jazzy and Gazebo Harmonic; all work runs in simulation.
